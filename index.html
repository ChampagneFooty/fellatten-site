The story so far

About DRAFT

Every once in a while, along comes a band so revolutionary it changes everything. Fell At Ten is not that band. But we're still pretty cool.

Formed in 2011, Fell At Ten started in the garage of singer and bassist Jake Daulby in little ol' Adelaide. Guitarist Timmy Raspe and drummer Darren Chalker answered an ad looking to start a band, and Darren went on to drive the band from behind the kit for seven and a half years. That garage became a melting pot of alt rock and sonic exploration: sweet harmonies, thumping beats and the odd scream.

We put out the Bamboozled EP in 2014, the single The Man Who Rescued A Bear From Drowning in 2016, and our debut album Another Day on New Year's Day 2018: fourteen tracks of heavy riffs, pop-punk drums and hidden easter eggs. triple j Unearthed played Ever After. Then Darren played his last gig at The Jade in August 2018 after seven and a half years as our drummer, Anthony Sherwood took over the kit, co-wrote two songs and played our last two shows, then Jake moved to the Central Coast in 2019 and the band went quiet. Sherwood didn't leave; we stopped. He'd come to us from Skeleton Club and kept going with Dojo Rise.

Now we're back with an acoustic rework of The River, a run of new releases through 2027, and a 10-year anniversary edition of Another Day.

History

  1. 2011

    Formed in Jake's garage in Penfield, SA, after Timmy and drummer Darren answered an ad.

  2. Mar 2014

    Debut EP Bamboozled: five tracks, "short, fast and loud".

  3. 2014–15

    Live at Adelaide College of the Arts and The Bridgeway.

  4. Sep 2016

    Single and video: The Man Who Rescued A Bear From Drowning. The Jade, 11 Nov.

  5. 2017

    Ever After on triple j Unearthed. The Colonel Light Hotel, 18 Mar.

  6. 1 Jan 2018

    Debut album Another Day, 14 tracks.

  7. Aug 2018

    Darren, our drummer since day one, plays his final gig at The Jade after seven and a half years.

  8. Late 2018

    Anthony Sherwood, from Skeleton Club, joins on drums, helps write two new songs and plays our last two shows.

  9. Dec 2018

    Support for Audio Reign's album launch at HQ Complex, 7 Dec.

  10. Jan 2019

    Farewell show at the Duke of York on 9 Jan before Jake moves to the Central Coast, NSW.

  11. 2027

    Back, with The River in March.
